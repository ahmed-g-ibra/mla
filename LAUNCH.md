# Launch checklist

The site is built. What follows is what stands between it and a legal first
sale. Items in the first section are blocking: the site will tell on itself
until they are done, because `launchBlockers()` in `src/lib/store-config.ts`
reads them.

## Blocking — you must do these

### 1. Replace the photographs
All 262 images in `public/products/` were taken from the public product feeds
of Egyptian Imports, Statue.com and The Ancient Home and belong to them. They
are here so the site could be built and reviewed against real content. Selling
against them is trading on someone else's work, and this repository documents
exactly where they came from.

The swap is a drop-in: same paths, same numbering, `public/products/<slug>/<n>.jpg`.
See `scripts/README.md`. Five photographs per piece, square, on a plain ground.

### 2. Price the catalogue
Current prices are scraped US retail from three competitors, converted from USD.
There is no cost basis behind them. Before launch you need, per piece: unit cost
from the workshop, inbound freight to Oman, customs duty, your margin, and then
the VAT-inclusive retail price. Prices live in `priceUsd` in
`src/data/products.ts` and are regenerated by `scripts/`.

Note the promises the site currently makes, which your pricing has to fund:
free GCC delivery, duties absorbed, and free return collection.

### 3. Fill in the company
`src/lib/store-config.ts`. Every field marked PLACEHOLDER is rendered into the
Terms page and will be read by a customer or a payment processor:

- `legalName` — the registered company name
- `crNumber` — commercial registration from MOCIIP
- `vatNumber` — Oman Tax Authority VAT identification
- `address` — registered address, English and Arabic
- `whatsappNumber` — digits only, international format, no `+`
- `email`

Until `whatsappNumber` changes from `971500000000`, **no order can reach you**,
and the Organization structured data deliberately publishes no contact details
rather than a false phone number.

### 4. Have the legal pages reviewed
`src/content/legal.ts` holds Terms of Sale, Privacy and Returns, written for an
Omani seller shipping across the GCC. They describe what this site actually
does, in plain language. They are a working draft, not legal advice — have a
lawyer in Oman read them, particularly the returns window and the consumer
protection references.

### 5. Register for VAT if you cross the threshold
Oman VAT is 5% and the site shows prices VAT-inclusive, as required. Mandatory
registration begins at OMR 38,500 of annual supplies; voluntary registration is
available from OMR 19,250. Confirm current thresholds with the Oman Tax
Authority — they change.

## Before you announce it

- [ ] Buy a domain and point it at Vercel. Then update `SITE_URL` in
      `src/lib/seo.ts`, which drives canonicals, hreflang, the sitemap and
      every structured-data URL.
- [ ] Google Search Console: verify the domain, submit `/sitemap.xml`, and
      confirm both `/en` and `/ar` are indexing.
- [ ] Test the WhatsApp flow end to end on a real phone, in both languages.
- [ ] Check a real order message arrives readable on an Arabic keyboard phone.
- [ ] Agree courier rates for all six GCC states and confirm the delivery
      estimates on `/shipping` are honest.
- [ ] Decide how you take money. Tap Payments and Checkout.com both onboard
      Omani merchants; both need the CR and VAT numbers above. The cart already
      produces a complete `Order` record in `src/lib/order.ts` for this.

## Worth doing early

- [ ] Google Business Profile, if you have a physical presence.
- [ ] Instagram linked in `store-config.ts` — it appears in structured data
      once the contact details are real.
- [ ] Photograph pieces at a consistent scale with a known object in frame.
      The site already tells customers the height in everyday terms, which is
      the commonest cause of a return on decorative goods.

## What is already done

Static site, 147 prerendered pages, English and Arabic with full RTL, seven
currencies with Oman as the default, VAT shown inclusive and broken out at
checkout, search across both languages, WhatsApp ordering with order
references, Product/Offer/FAQ/Breadcrumb/ItemList structured data, canonicals
and hreflang throughout, cookieless analytics, the brand lockup wired through
chrome, favicon and structured data, and WCAG AA contrast across every text
pair.
